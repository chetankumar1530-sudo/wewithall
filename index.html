Frequently Asked Questions

How does the placement guarantee work?+

If you don't receive a job offer within 6 months of completing the program, we either refund 50% of your fees or help you launch your own startup with full incorporation support. Our placement rate is 92%.

Do I need prior coding experience?+

No! Our program starts from absolute basics. Module 1 covers Python from scratch. Many of our successful students came from non-tech backgrounds like commerce, arts, and biology.

What kind of startups can I launch?+

We support AI/ML-based startups across all sectors — healthcare, fintech, edtech, agritech, SaaS, and more. Our team helps you identify market opportunities and build an MVP.

How do you help with fundraising?+

We provide pitch deck templates, financial modeling tools, access to our investor database of 500+ angels and VCs, and warm introductions through our network.

Is the program online or offline?+

We offer both! Our hybrid model includes live online classes, recorded lectures, and optional in-person workshops in Bangalore, Mumbai, and Delhi NCR.

What makes We With All different?+

Unlike traditional institutes, we don't just teach — we guarantee outcomes. Plus our startup launchpad is unique: no other institute helps you incorporate a company and raise funding if you choose the entrepreneurial path.
