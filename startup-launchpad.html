Choose Your Structure

We help you select the right business structure based on your goals, funding needs, and liability preferences.

Private Limited Company

Best for startups seeking VC funding

  • Min 2 Directors, 2 Shareholders
  • Limited liability protection
  • Easier to raise investment
  • Higher compliance requirements
LLP

Ideal for professional services & small teams

  • Min 2 Partners
  • Lower compliance burden
  • Flexible partnership agreement
  • Limited fundraising options
One Person Company (OPC)

Perfect for solo founders testing ideas

  • Single founder ownership
  • Nominee director required
  • Simplified compliance
  • Can convert to Pvt Ltd later

Tax Registrations

  • GST Registration: Mandatory for turnover > ₹20L (₹10L for NE states). We file monthly/quarterly returns.
  • MSME/Udyam Registration: Benefits in government tenders, lower interest loans, and tax rebates.
  • IEC (Import Export Code): Required if you plan to sell products/services globally.
  • Professional Tax: State-specific registration for employees.
🛡️

Intellectual Property

  • Trademark Registration: Protect your brand name, logo, and tagline. We handle TM search, Class selection, and filing.
  • Copyright Registration: Protect your code, content, and creative work.
  • Patent Filing: If you've built novel AI/ML algorithms, we connect you with patent attorneys.
  • NDA Templates: Ready-to-use Non-Disclosure Agreements for vendors, employees, and investors.

Ongoing Compliance

Co-founder Agreement

The most important document for any startup. We draft a comprehensive agreement covering:

  • Equity split & vesting schedule (standard 4-year vesting, 1-year cliff)
  • Roles & responsibilities of each founder
  • IP assignment – all code/IP belongs to the company
  • Decision-making & dispute resolution
  • Exit clauses – what happens if a founder leaves
  • Non-compete & confidentiality clauses
💎
